About Me

I am currently working at Samsung Research as a Research Engineer. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from KAIST, where I was advised by Prof. Junmo Kim. During my Ph.D., I worked on efficient AI, including network pruning, token pruning, and test-time adaptation, and on LLM safety, including unlearning and red-teaming. These days I am mainly interested in LLM agents, and more broadly in building AI systems that are efficient, reliable, and practical to deploy.
